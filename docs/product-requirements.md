# Product requirements

**Vision:** Invest in Africa from anywhere. One investment account for Africans everywhere.

**First corridor:** Ghanaian living in Sweden. SEK → FX → GHS → Ghana investment → portfolio.

**First market:** Ghana (GSE sandbox). Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, BRVM and global markets are architected and labelled honestly.

**Non-goals for MVP:** live brokerage, live payments, claimed licences, dark-pattern growth, crypto casino UX, rewriting for each country.

**Quality bar:** Revolut / Avanza / Wise as inspiration for craft, not copies. Mobile-first. Premium, calm, African and international.
